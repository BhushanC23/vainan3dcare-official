Curriculum Modules

WORKSHOP TOPICS

Four intensive modules designed to bridge the gap between radiological diagnostic imaging and precision CAD/CAM laboratory execution.

MODULE 01 Diagnostic Imaging

1. Understanding and Interpreting CBCT / OPG

Mastering the systematic analysis of 3D cone-beam computed tomography and panoramic radiography. Evaluating alveolar bone density, cortical thickness, nerve canal tracing, maxillary sinus morphology, and pathosis identification.

  • • DICOM dataset navigation and multi-planar cross-sectional evaluation
  • • Inferior alveolar nerve (IAN) tracing & safety margins
  • • Identifying anatomic anomalies and bone graft requirements
MODULE 02 Surgical Methodology

2. Fundamentals of Implant Dentistry from a Guided Surgery Perspective

Transitioning from freehand intuition to mathematically guided implant osteotomy. Biomechanics of guided drilling, sleeve offset calculations, temperature control, and tissue-friendly flapless protocol execution.

  • • Flapless vs. min-flap biological considerations and healing times
  • • Drill sequence calibration and mechanical tolerance handling
  • • Risk management and primary stability optimization
MODULE 03 Digital CAD Planning

3. Implant Planning and Surgical Guide Designing

Hands-on digital CAD session merging patient optical .STL scans with radiographic .DCM datasets. Designing tooth-borne, mucosa-borne, and bone-borne surgical templates with retention pin stabilization.

  • • Merging accuracy verification and artifact elimination
  • • Virtual tooth setup and crown-down prosthetic planning
  • • Generating ready-to-print 3D resin & metal guide geometries
MODULE 04 Restorative Execution

4. Designing and Creating Prosthetics via Digital Pathways

Connecting surgical placement with restorative perfection. Designing immediate load screw-retained provisionals, customized Ti Base abutments, monolithic zirconia crowns, and Malo framework superstructures.

  • • Virtual emergence profile modeling and soft-tissue conditioning
  • • Multi-unit abutment (MUA) angling and framework passivity
  • • Direct CAM manufacturing and ceramic finishing integration
