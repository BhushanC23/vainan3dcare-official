Integrated Orthodontic Pathway

DIGITAL WORKFLOW

Scanning → Designing → Manufacturing. Every case is digitally orchestrated to ensure biological safety, optimal tooth movement speed, and clinical predictability.

1

Scanning

Clinicians submit digital intraoral scans (Medit, 3Shape, iTero, etc.) or physical silicone impressions. Scans are checked for arch accuracy and occlusion.

2

Designing

Orthodontist-led treatment planning and staging. Movement paths, attachment placement, and IPR requirements are simulated and exported as an educational 3D video.

3

Manufacturing

High-precision direct 3D printing or model thermoforming, laser etching of stage numbers, ultrasonic cleaning, and hermetic custom box packaging.
