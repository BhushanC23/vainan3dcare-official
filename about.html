Our Origins

COMPANY HISTORY

VAINAN 3D CARE PRIVATE LIMITED was incorporated in July, 2022 and started its operations in the October month of the same year.

From metal 3D printing roots to a comprehensive dental prostheses solutions provider—our evolution has been swift.

We combine advanced Artificial Intelligence with Metal 3D Printing and Precision Milling to craft dental prostheses including crowns, bridges, implant superstructures, aligners, and surgical stents and models for mock surgeries.
